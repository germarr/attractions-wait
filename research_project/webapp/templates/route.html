Magic Kingdom — route optimizer

Maximize attractions visited in one day. Time-dependent orienteering over OpenStreetMap walking distances and 91 days of hourly wait history.

1  Which attractions will you not leave without?

Pick up to 5, then put them in order of importance. The planner treats these as fixed commitments and schedules them where their queues are cheapest — everything else is fitted around them. If they cannot all fit on your date, the lowest-ranked one is dropped first, which is why the order matters.

    2  When do you want to eat?

    The plan keeps this window completely free of queueing rather than assuming you can eat while in line. It costs time but no walking — quick service is spread across the park.

    3  Fireworks and the parade?

    Being somewhere at a fixed time is a real constraint on the rest of the day, so the planner routes you to the viewing spot and holds you there. Each shows how well we know its start time.

    The three sliders are the model's invented constants. Move them to see how much the answer depends on them.
